Privacy Policy

Application: Zaid Personal Automation

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Purpose and scope

This is a private, self-hosted application used by its owner to run selected Google account automations. It is not offered to the general public.

Google user data accessed

Depending on the workflow authorized by the owner, the application may access basic Google account identity, YouTube channel information and video-upload permissions, and Gmail messages or metadata required by configured email workflows. It requests only the OAuth permissions needed for those functions.

How data is used

YouTube data is used to select the intended channel, upload original videos, and verify upload status and privacy. Gmail data is used only by the owner's explicitly configured email workflows. Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

Storage, retention, and security

OAuth credentials and workflow state are stored in the owner's local, self-hosted environment and are excluded from source control. Operational logs retain only the information needed to diagnose workflows and prevent duplicate actions. Credentials are retained until the owner disconnects the account, deletes the local credential, or revokes access through Google Account settings.

Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers. Data is sent to Google APIs only as required to perform the requested Google action. It may be disclosed only when required by law or necessary to protect the security of the owner's account and systems.

User control and deletion

The owner can pause workflows, delete locally stored credentials and workflow records, or revoke the application's access from the Google Account connections page.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
